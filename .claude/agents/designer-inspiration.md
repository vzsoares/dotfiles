# Designer inspiration bases

Where the designer looks when it builds a shortlist of references (rule 9 in
`designer.md`). Pick concrete pages from these, not the base's front page.
References are studied, never copied (rule 3). Access: free, login, paid.
Only the user adds bases; keep one line each.

## Landing and marketing sites

- Awwwards (awwwards.com): award-winning sites, filter by tech and category. Free.
- Godly (godly.website): tight curation of striking modern sites. Free.
- Land-book (land-book.com): landing pages by section type (hero, pricing, footer). Free.
- Lapa Ninja (lapa.ninja): landing pages grouped by industry. Free.
- SaaSPO (saaspo.com): SaaS landing pages broken into sections. Free.
- Httpster (httpster.net): bold, unusual layouts. Free.
- Dribbble (dribbble.com): shots for mood and direction, less for real flows. Free; login to save.

## Product UI and app flows

- Mobbin (mobbin.com): real app screens and full flows, web and mobile. Free tier; paid for all.
- Page Flows (pageflows.com): recorded user flows (onboarding, checkout). Paid; free samples.
- Refero (refero.design): searchable real product screens. Free tier; login.
- Screenlane (screenlane.com): mobile and web UI patterns by screen type. Free.
- Behance (behance.net): case studies with process, good for dashboards and brand-to-UI. Free.
- Collect UI (collectui.com): UI pieces by challenge (forms, charts, settings). Free.

## Design systems

- Design Systems Gallery (designsystems.surf): index of public systems with links. Free.
- Storybook showcase (storybook.js.org/showcase): live component libraries to inspect. Free.
- Material 3 (m3.material.io), Carbon (carbondesignsystem.com), Polaris (polaris.shopify.com), Primer (primer.style): documented systems for rules on states, density, a11y. Free.
- Radix Themes / shadcn/ui (radix-ui.com/themes, ui.shadcn.com): token and component structure worth reading. Free.
- VoltAgent/awesome-claude-design (github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-claude-design): catalogue of brand-derived `DESIGN.md` files. Reference only: name a bar from it, never drop one in. Free.

## Typography, color, motion

- Fonts In Use (fontsinuse.com): real typefaces in real work. Free.
- Fontshare (fontshare.com), Google Fonts (fonts.google.com), Fontsource: commercially licensed families; record the license. Free.
- Typewolf (typewolf.com): trending pairings and what sites use. Free.
- Coolors (coolors.co), Realtime Colors (realtimecolors.com): palettes, and previewing them on a real page. Free; login to save.
- Radix Colors (radix-ui.com/colors): accessible 12-step scales, light and dark. Free.
- Motion (motion.dev), Lottie Files (lottiefiles.com): easing and animation patterns; check the license per asset. Free tier.
- Codrops (tympanus.net/codrops): scroll and interaction experiments to study, not to paste. Free.
